#### 1. Introduction
This privacy policy explains how s.n.u ("we", "this website") handles information about visitors to this website.

#### 2. Analytics
We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how this website is used and to improve its content.
- **Cookies:** Google Analytics uses cookies to collect data.
- **Data collected:** The data does not include information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address. Your IP address is sent to Google when you view a page, but according to Google, Google Analytics (GA4) does not log or store IP addresses.
- **Visitors from Europe:** If you visit from Europe (EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland, etc.), we only use Google Analytics after you click "Accept" in the cookie notice. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. <a href="#" onclick="snuResetConsent();return false;">Change cookie settings</a>
- **Opting out:** You can block cookies in your browser settings, or use the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **More information:** See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time using the link above.

#### 3. Information sent to third parties

| **Recipient** | **Information** | **Purpose** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google LLC** (Google Analytics) | Pages viewed (URL, time), device information (browser type, screen resolution, etc.), IP address, referrer, identifier stored in a cookie | Analysing use of this website and improving it |
| **SSGform** (only when you submit the contact form) | What you enter in the form (name, email address, message), time of submission, IP address | Delivering your message to us |

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

#### 4. Personal information
Information you send us through the contact form (name, email address, message, etc.) is handled as follows.
- **Purpose:** To reply to your inquiry and contact you by email when needed.
- **Sharing:** We do not provide your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
- **Contact form service:** The contact form uses the form service [SSGform](https://ssgform.com/). Your input is delivered to us through this service.
- **Security:** Personal information is kept in an environment that only people who need it for our work can access, protected by passwords and similar measures. Information that is no longer needed after we have finished handling your inquiry is deleted.
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** Taking steps at your request and our legitimate interest in replying to inquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

#### 5. Your rights
You can ask us to tell you how we use your personal information, to give you access to it, or to correct, delete, or stop using it. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to data portability, the right to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To make a request, please contact us using the form below. We will respond without undue delay after confirming your identity, free of charge.
